2. Descriptive Title of the Invention

AI-Based Traffic Congestion Prediction and Dynamic Signal Control System

3. Abstract of the Invention (100 words or less)

The invention relates to an intelligent traffic management system that uses artificial intelligence and real-time sensor data (cameras, IoT sensors and GPS/vehicle probe data) to predict traffic congestion before it occurs and dynamically control traffic signal timings accordingly. A machine learning/deep learning model analyses historical and live traffic patterns, weather and event data to forecast congestion at intersections and road segments. Based on these predictions, an adaptive signal control module automatically adjusts green-light duration, phase sequence and cycle length across coordinated intersections in real time, reducing waiting time, fuel consumption and emissions while improving overall traffic flow and road safety.

4. Detailed Description of the Invention

a. Problem the invention is solving:
Conventional traffic signals operate on fixed or pre-timed schedules and cannot adapt to real-time fluctuations in traffic volume. This leads to unnecessary congestion, increased travel time, fuel wastage, pollution and higher accident risk, especially during peak hours, festivals, accidents or other unplanned events.

b. Technical features of the invention:
The system comprises: (i) a multi-source data acquisition layer using CCTV cameras, inductive-loop/IoT sensors, GPS-based vehicle probe data and weather feeds; (ii) a data pre-processing and feature-extraction module; (iii) an AI/deep-learning prediction engine (e.g., XGBoost, Random Forest, LSTM/GRU) that forecasts congestion at each intersection/road segment in advance; (iv) an adaptive dynamic signal control algorithm that computes the optimal green time, phase sequence and offset for each junction bounded by strict safety invariants (minimum green: 10s, maximum green: 60s, yellow: 4s, all-red: 2s); (v) an edge/cloud computing architecture with vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication; (vi) a central dashboard showing real-time congestion heat-maps and alerts; and (vii) a fail-safe fallback to standard fixed-time operation if the system or connectivity fails.

c. General Utility/application of the invention:
Applicable to urban traffic intersections and corridors, smart-city traffic management systems, highway entry/toll points, emergency-vehicle priority routing, and event or stadium traffic management.

d. Advantages of the invention:
Reduces average vehicle waiting and travel time (by over 40%), lowers fuel consumption and vehicular emissions, improves intersection throughput (+19.2%), reduces congestion-related accidents, enables green-corridor priority for emergency vehicles, scales to a city-wide network of intersections, and reduces dependence on manual traffic-police intervention.

e. Best way of using the invention as well as possible variants:
The primary embodiment is deployed at urban intersections using camera and loop sensors feeding an edge controller or central AI server that coordinates signal timing across a network of junctions. Variants include: use of only smartphone/GPS crowd-sourced data for low-infrastructure regions; integration of an emergency-vehicle detection and priority module; and an edge-AI variant that performs inference locally at the signal controller cabinet for low-latency operation without dependence on continuous cloud connectivity.

6. Existing State-of-the-Art and Prior Arts

Present-day traffic control largely relies on fixed-time signal plans or reactive adaptive systems (e.g., SCOOT/SCATS) that adjust timing based on currently detected congestion using loop detectors, and on manual control by traffic police at busy junctions. These approaches respond only after congestion has already formed and typically optimise a single intersection in isolation, rather than predicting congestion in advance and coordinating signal timing across a network of intersections.

8. Technical Features and Elements of the Invention

Key technical features/elements: (i) an AI/deep-learning based congestion-prediction module; (ii) multi-sensor data fusion combining camera, loop/IoT sensor and GPS probe data; (iii) a dynamic signal-timing optimisation engine; (iv) cloud/edge computing infrastructure with V2I communication; (v) a central real-time monitoring dashboard; (vi) an emergency-vehicle detection and priority module; and (vii) a fail-safe manual-override and stale-data fallback mechanism.

9. Novel Features Distinguishing the Invention from Prior Art

The invention is believed to be new in combining: (a) predictive (rather than merely reactive) congestion forecasting using machine learning; (b) fusion of camera, IoT-sensor, GPS and weather data; (c) network-wide coordinated signal optimisation rather than single-intersection control; (d) self-learning closed loop that continuously evaluates live traffic performance; and (e) integrated emergency-vehicle priority within the same predictive-control framework with strict safety invariants.

10. Prototype Implementation & Test Verification

YES. Built, tested, and functionally verified. A complete working prototype simulation system was designed, built, and tested by the inventors as part of their final-year B.Tech project in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, AKGEC, Ghaziabad, under the guidance of Mr. Mahesh Kumar Sharma. The prototype demonstrated:

  • 40.8% reduction in average vehicle waiting time compared to fixed-time control.
  • 41.2% reduction in average queue lengths across all intersection arms.
  • +19.2% increase in vehicle throughput during comparable traffic load.
  • 92.8% ML classification accuracy achieved by the selected XGBoost engine.
Full source code, simulation engine, and test datasets are maintained by the inventors.

11. Conception of the Idea

The idea was conceived jointly by the inventors during final-year project discussions in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College, Ghaziabad, after observing that fixed-time traffic signals at urban intersections could not adapt to real-time traffic demand, leading to avoidable congestion, waiting time and fuel wastage. Upon reviewing existing adaptive traffic-control systems and recent AI/ML literature on traffic forecasting, the inventors formulated the concept of combining predictive, deep-learning based congestion forecasting with a dynamic signal-control system, under the guidance of their supervisor, Mr. Mahesh Kumar Sharma.

13. Reasons Invention is Non-Obvious

Combining predictive, multi-horizon congestion forecasting with a dynamic signal controller governed by deterministic safety invariants is not a straightforward extension of existing rule-based adaptive systems such as SCOOT/SCATS. Prior systems react only after congestion has formed, whereas this invention pre-emptively adjusts signal plans before congestion occurs. This requires a non-trivial fusion of heterogeneous real-time data streams and a novel closed-loop prediction-control architecture that a person of ordinary skill would not arrive at without inventive effort.

14. Hardware Components Involved in the Architecture

CCTV cameras and image sensors; inductive-loop/ultrasonic/IoT traffic sensors; GPS-enabled probe vehicles/smartphones; edge/fog computing unit (industrial PC/single-board computer) at each intersection; central cloud server hosting the AI prediction and signal-coordination engine; cellular (4G/5G)/fiber network; IoT relay/control interface to signal controller heads; and uninterruptible power backup.

15. Societal, Environmental & Economic Benefits

The invention reduces commuter travel time and fuel wastage, lowers vehicular greenhouse gas emissions, reduces congestion-related rear-end accidents, enables faster emergency response through green corridors for ambulances and fire trucks, and supports smart-city sustainable urban-mobility initiatives.

16. Any Other Information

The system can be integrated with existing Smart City Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) infrastructure and can be extended in future work to incorporate vehicle-to-vehicle/vehicle-to-everything (V2V/V2X) communication with autonomous vehicles for further optimisation.
